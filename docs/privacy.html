Privacy Policy

Spoonlet for iOS · last updated 15 August 2026

The short version: Spoonlet has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you record stays in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing about you or your child is transmitted anywhere unless you deliberately export or share it yourself.

What Spoonlet stores

Spoonlet records, on your device only:

  • Your child's name, date of birth, sex and — if you enter it — weeks of gestation at birth.
  • Feeds you log: time, type, amount in millilitres or grams, foods, how the meal went, and any reaction you note.
  • Growth measurements: weight, length and head circumference.
  • Sleep and nappy entries.
  • Any amounts or introduction ages you enter from your paediatrician, along with the note naming who prescribed them.
  • Your app preferences: units, appearance and which reminders are on.

What Spoonlet does not do

  • There is no account and no sign-in. Spoonlet never asks who you are.
  • There is no server. Spoonlet has no backend to send anything to.
  • There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, because nothing is collected.

When your device does contact the internet

Two things reach outside the app, and both only when you choose them:

  • Opening a source. Tapping a citation opens the guideline or study in your browser. That is an ordinary visit to who.int, doi.org, aap.org and similar, subject to those sites' own policies. Spoonlet sends nothing about you or your child along with it.
  • Exporting your data. Settings → Export as JSON writes a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice.

Notifications

Reminders are off by default and are scheduled locally by your device. They are never delivered through a push server, and their content is composed on your device from your own data.

Children's data

Spoonlet is a tool for parents and carers, not an app for children to use. It is not directed to children and is not listed in the App Store Kids Category. Because Spoonlet collects nothing and transmits nothing, no information about a child is ever received by us or anyone else.

Deleting everything

Settings → Delete all data removes every entry for that child from the device immediately. Deleting the app removes all remaining data. There is no copy anywhere else to request the deletion of.

Medical disclaimer

Spoonlet summarises published infant-feeding guidance from bodies including the WHO, ESPGHAN, the AAP and NIAID, and cites its sources. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and it cannot examine your child. Always consult your paediatrician before making decisions about feeding, supplements or allergen introduction, and seek urgent care for breathing difficulty, facial swelling, repeated vomiting after a food, or a baby who stops feeding.

Changes

If a future version ever transmits data — optional iCloud sync is written but switched off — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy labels will change with it.
