Tummi

Baby feeding, with the reasoning attached.

Most baby apps do one of two things. Some explain what to introduce and how to cut it safely, but refuse to count anything. Others count millilitres and plot growth curves, but never say a word about what to feed or why. Tummi does both, and names its sources for all of it.

What it does

  • Tracks feeds in grams and millilitres, and compares them to the published range for your baby's exact age — not a generic target.
  • 148 foods with the age each is usually introduced, allergen status, choking risk and how to cut and serve it at 6–8 months and again from 9 months. Plus 20 things to hold off on, each with the reason.
  • Plots weight, length and head circumference on the real WHO Child Growth Standards.
  • Notices which of the top-9 allergens were introduced once and then quietly dropped — the failure mode the LEAP and EAT trials warn about.
  • Every recommendation carries a tappable citation. Eighteen sources, listed in full in the app.
  • Your paediatrician's amounts and introduction ages override the published ones, with their name attached — while the guidance stays visible alongside.
Privacy: no account, no server, no analytics. Everything stays on your device. Full privacy policy.

Not medical advice

Tummi summarises published infant-feeding guidance and cites it. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and it cannot examine your child. Always consult your paediatrician before making decisions about feeding, supplements or allergen introduction.

Tummi is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the World Health Organization, ESPGHAN, the American Academy of Pediatrics, NIAID or any other body whose guidance it cites.
